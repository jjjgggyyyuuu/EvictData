# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 08, 2025

## Introduction

Eviction Management System ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our eviction management platform.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Fill out forms or fields through our service
- Participate in surveys
- Send us communication
- Use certain features of our platform

This may include:
- Contact information (name, email address, phone number, mailing address)
- Account credentials
- Financial information (payment method details)
- Property information
- Tenant information
- Eviction case details
- Documents related to eviction proceedings
- Communication records

### Automatically Collected Information

When you access our platform, we may automatically collect:
- Device information (such as your mobile device ID, model, and manufacturer)
- IP address
- Browser type
- Operating system
- Device's location
- Access times and dates
- Pages viewed
- Other usage data

## How We Use Your Information

We may use your information for:
- Providing and maintaining our service
- Processing and completing transactions
- Fulfilling our contractual obligations
- Sending administrative information
- Responding to inquiries
- Improving user experience
- Protecting against fraudulent or illegal activity
- Complying with legal obligations
- Marketing and promotional purposes (with your consent)

## Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your information with:
- Service providers who perform services on our behalf

- Law enforcement or government entities when required by law
- Other third parties with your consent

## Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure
- Right to restrict processing

## Children's Information

Our service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it.
