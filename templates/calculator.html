About the Eviction Process

1
Filing

The eviction process begins when the landlord files an eviction case with the court.

2
Hearing

A court hearing is scheduled, typically within 7-14 days of filing. Both landlord and tenant present their case.

3
Judgment

If the court rules in favor of the landlord, a judgment for possession is issued.

4
Writ of Possession

The landlord can request a writ of possession, which authorizes the sheriff to remove the tenant.

5
Eviction

The sheriff executes the writ by physically removing the tenant if they haven't already vacated.

Average Timelines

  • Filing to Hearing 10 days
  • Hearing to Judgment 3 days
  • Judgment to Writ 7 days
  • Writ to Eviction 7 days
  • Total Process 27 days
